Lesson Booking & Hour Tracking

Give students a self-serve way to book instructors, and give your office a live view of who's close to test-ready. Every completed session updates the student's hour count automatically — no manual spreadsheets.

What's included

  • ● Real-time instructor and vehicle availability
  • ● Automatic completed-vs-required hour tracking per license type
