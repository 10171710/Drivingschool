Common questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How long does it take to get a license? +
Most students complete the full program in 8–12 weeks. This includes written test prep (1–2 weeks), permit processing, 30 logged practice hours, and road test preparation. The timeline depends on your availability and how quickly you book lessons.
What's included in the $899 manual course? +
Everything: written test prep materials, learner's permit application support, 30 hours of dual-control car instruction, highway and night driving modules, mock road tests, and booking assistance for your official road test. No hidden fees.
Can I log practice hours with a family member? +
Yes — up to 15 of your 30 required hours can be supervised by a licensed family member over 21. The remaining 15 must be with a certified instructor. All hours are logged through our app with date, time, and supervisor signature.
What happens if I fail the road test? +
We offer a free re-test preparation session and discounted re-booking. Your instructor will review what went wrong, schedule extra practice on those specific skills, and re-book your test. 94% of our students pass on the first attempt.
Do you offer weekend lessons? +
Yes — lessons run 7 days a week, including weekends and evenings. Weekend slots fill up fast, so we recommend booking at least a week in advance. You can manage all bookings through the student dashboard.
