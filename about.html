About us

Built by people who missed too many appointment windows themselves

RouteWise started in 2019 as a booking tool for one driving school. Six years later it runs lessons, license hours and instructor feedback for driving academies around the world.

Our mission

Make expert time easy to book, and easy to prove

Learning to drive lives and dies on trust — did the student get their hours, did the instructor log the session, did the progress match the plan. We build the paper trail so nobody has to ask.

Every RouteWise academy, from a solo instructor to a 40-instructor driving school, runs on the same core: a shared calendar, a student record, and a clear line back to test day.
